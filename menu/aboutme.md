Hi, I'm Liutao, a non-tenured research associate (特任副研究员) affiliated with University of Science and Technology of China. I graduated from PolyU, Hong Kong. My advisors are [Dr. WU Shining](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/lms/people/academic-staff/shining-wu/) and [Prof. JIANG Li](https://bs.ustc.edu.cn/chinese/profile-2563.html). My area of interest is Operations Management Modelling.
